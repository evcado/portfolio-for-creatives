    Branding

    Packaging

    Meraki was created as a fictional Greek wine brand with a goal of providing a unique and memorable experience. Meraki connects wine consumers to the Greek mythology with an immersive storytelling experience.

    • Packaging

    Each wine bottle is designed to represent a different Greek God, and is thoughtfully paired with a wine that reflects the gods characteristics and personality type. Dionysus, the god of wine and festivities, is the face of the brand, his lavish lifestyle is reflected in the brands elegant style. Dionysus is often associated with panthers, which became the inspiration for the brands logo. with the face of Dionysus careful carved into the white space.
